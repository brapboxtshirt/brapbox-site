Questions

What is DTF printing?

DTF stands for direct to film. The design is printed onto a film and heat-pressed onto the garment. We use it for orders of 12 to 24 pieces.

What's your smallest order?

12 pieces. For 12 to 24 pieces we do a DTF print, and from 25 pieces up we screen print.

Can I switch to screen printing later?

Yes. Once your order reaches 25 pieces we screen print it, and we confirm the final method after reviewing your design.
